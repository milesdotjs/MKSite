About Muddy Paws

Mobile dog grooming that comes to you

Our story

Hi, I'm Dan. I started Muddy Paws in 2021 with a second-hand van, a dog bath I fitted myself (badly, the first time) and a cocker spaniel called Biscuit who hated every grooming salon we ever tried.

The idea was simple: if the salon is the problem, bring the salon to the dog. Three years and a frankly unbelievable amount of fur later, the van is a proper mobile grooming room with a heated bath, a hydraulic table and a very loud dryer. Biscuit still supervises.

I cover Bristol, Bath and most of the villages in between. Not sure if I reach you? Ring 07700 900456 and ask. I probably do.
