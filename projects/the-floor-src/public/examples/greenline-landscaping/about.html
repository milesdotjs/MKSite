About Greenline Landscaping

Garden makeovers and paving, done properly

Our story

I started Greenline in 2014 with a van, a wheelbarrow and a lot of borrowed tools. The first couple of years were mostly lawns and hedges. Then a neighbour asked if I could lay a patio, and it turned out that was the bit I liked most.

These days we're a crew of three. We do full garden makeovers, patios and paths, new lawns, fencing, and the regular hedge and border work that keeps a garden looking its best. Most of our jobs come from people who have seen one of our gardens over a fence, and we'd like to keep it that way.

If you're not sure what you want yet, that's fine. We'll come round, have a look and talk it through. The quote is free and there's no pressure.
