Our services

Here's what we can do for you.

Annual membership

Join the Players for a year. Covers rehearsals, insurance and a vote at the AGM. Under-18s and students pay half.

£25 a year

Show tickets

Adults £10, concessions £8, under-16s £5. Available on the door or by phone from a fortnight before each show.

From £5

Friends of Riverside Players

Support us without joining. Friends receive two tickets to every show and a thank-you in the programme.

£60 a year

Volunteering

Set building, costumes, lighting, front of house and the tea urn. No experience and no membership needed to help out.

Free

Young Players

Our youth group for ages 8 to 16, Saturday mornings in term time. They put on their own show every July.

£3 a session
