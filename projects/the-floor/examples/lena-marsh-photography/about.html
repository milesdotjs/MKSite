About Lena Marsh Photography

Weddings and families, photographed honestly

Our story

I'm Lena. I've been photographing weddings since 2017, after a guest at a friend's wedding saw my holiday photos and asked me to shoot hers. I said yes before I'd thought about it, and I've been saying yes ever since.

I shoot on two cameras, avoid flash where I can, and would rather catch your grandmother laughing than line everyone up on the church steps (we'll do the steps too, quickly). Between weddings I photograph families outdoors, usually with a dog involved.

I'm based at Paintworks in Bristol and happy to travel anywhere in the South West, and further for the right day.
