For developers

This tool doesn't take your job. It takes the version of the job where someone charges two thousand dollars for a template.

Send prospective clients here before you quote. When they've seen what five minutes and no skill produces, your proposal has something to stand next to, and everything you list that isn't in the ZIP is visibly the thing they're paying for.

Link to it

Put this on your pricing page, your proposal template, or your email signature. Plain link, no badge, no tracking.

<a href="https://mileskingdev.com/projects/the-floor/">Here's the baseline. Here's what I add.</a>

Frame your pitch as baseline plus what you add

The Reality Check at the end of the wizard lists ten things a developer worth paying should cover, with the questions a client should ask. Answer those questions in your proposal before they ask. If your answer to any of them is "I don't do that", say so and say who does.

What this tool is and isn't

  • It's a deterministic template filler. No AI, no server, no accounts. The source is plain TypeScript.
  • It's not a website builder and it says so. Nobody should run their business on the output long term.
  • The output is meant to be respectable and hollow. If yours looks like it, that's the point being made.
